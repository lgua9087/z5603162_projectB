# MarketReady Funds report map

`report.docx` is the editable source and `report.pdf` is the submission copy.
The current build contains 10 pages of narrative, one references page, and six
appendix pages.

## Narrative pages

1. Cover and executive summary.
2. Product, target investor, customer journey, and data provenance.
3. Walk-forward construction, information clock, execution, and validation.
4. Out-of-sample performance table and investor-focused interpretation.
5. Growth-of-$1 comparison.
6. Drawdown comparison and loss-tolerance interpretation.
7. Combined-fund target weights, capital/risk contribution, concentration, and
   fact-sheet design.
8. VADER design, sector index, and cross-sectional sentiment validation.
9. Coverage-aware sentiment fusion before/after result and commercial meaning.
10. Live investor product, final look-through features, critical reflection,
    investor suitability, recommendations, and conclusion.

## Material excluded from the narrative limit

- Page 11: references, reproducibility basis, and live project links.
- Appendix A: Sharpe ranking for all 12 funds.
- Appendix B: latest holdings summary for every fund.
- Appendix C: 23 pipeline validations and retained source-integrity checks.
- Appendix D: annual fund results for 2021, 2022, and 2023.
- Appendix E: transaction-cost, management-fee, and sentiment-parameter
  sensitivity.
- Appendix F: VADER distribution audit, return-imputation quantification, and
  the documented optimiser fallback event.
